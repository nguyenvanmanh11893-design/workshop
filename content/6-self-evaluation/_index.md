Throughout my journey in the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program, I have gained valuable foundational knowledge and practical hands-on experience. This learning path was cultivated through proactive study of official AWS documentation, hands-on lab completions, attendance at in-depth technical sharing sessions, and maintaining a disciplined weekly worklog.

Over the course of the training roadmap, I completed essential modules covering core AWS services and architectures, including Identity and Access Management (IAM), Elastic Compute Cloud (EC2), Virtual Private Cloud (VPC), Simple Storage Service (S3), Relational Database Service (RDS), CloudWatch monitoring and alarms, Containers, Serverless architectures, as well as networking and cloud security best practices. Furthermore, participating in AWS Community Day events and real-world technical discussions helped me better understand how enterprises adopt cloud computing to solve complex business problems.

Beyond technical competencies, I progressively developed key professional skills such as architectural design thinking, technical documentation, problem diagnosis and resolution, collaborative teamwork, and persistent self-directed learning.

- **Elevate architectural design thinking**: Strictly apply the AWS Well-Architected Framework pillars, placing particular emphasis on Cost Optimization and Operational Reliability.
- **Accumulate more production-grade experience**: Actively build and experiment with multi-tier distributed applications to sharpen debugging and troubleshooting skills in complex environments.
- **Refine technical presentation and communication skills**: Enhance the ability to articulate technical ideas clearly, present architectural solutions, and demo features with confidence.
- **Maintain active community engagement**: Continuously exchange ideas with industry peers, learn from mentors, and share practical experiences within the AWS Study Group community.
